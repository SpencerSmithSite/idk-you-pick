Description
------------
Can't decide where to eat? Let IDK You Pick choose for you.

IDK You Pick is a fast, fun restaurant decision-making app for couples, groups, and anyone tired of the "I don't know, what do you want?" conversation.

FEATURES
• Random Choice — Get an instant restaurant pick with one tap.
• Head-to-Head — Compare two restaurants side-by-side and pick a winner.
• Smart Filters — Filter by cuisine (Italian, Mexican, BBQ, etc.), type (sit-down, fast casual, quick), and price tier.
• Nearby Mode — Uses your location to show restaurants closest to you.
• Favorites & History — Save winners and see where you've already been.
• Search & Sort — Full-text search with sorting by name, distance, price, or random.
• Onboarding — Quick first-launch tutorial shows how everything works.
• Beautiful Themes — Dark and light mode with a modern glass-card UI (Aurora Frost).
• Share — Send a restaurant to friends via messages or a shareable link.
• Accessibility Friendly — Supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and screen-reader semantics.

PRIVACY
IDK You Pick uses your location only when you enable Nearby Mode. Location data stays on your device and is never shared. Read our full Privacy Policy inside the app.

Built with Flutter in Upstate NY.
